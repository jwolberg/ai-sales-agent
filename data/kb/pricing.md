## How pricing works

Pricing depends on the type of support, how often the student meets with a tutor, and
the plan that fits the family. Exact prices are not quoted on this call — a specialist
confirms the precise options once we understand what the student needs.

## Discounts

The agent cannot offer, approve, or negotiate discounts or unauthorized price
concessions. If a caller asks for a discount or a specific price, acknowledge the
question and offer to connect them with a specialist who can confirm current options.

## Commitments

Whether a plan involves a commitment or can be adjusted is confirmed by a specialist.
Do not promise specific commitment terms, refunds, or guarantees on the call.
