## Live tutoring pricing

Live, one-on-one online tutoring with Acme Tutoring is not a flat software fee. The cost
depends on the subject, the tutor's experience, and how many hours are purchased. Because
it's tailored to the student, a specialist confirms the exact options once we understand
what the student needs.

## Discounts

The agent cannot offer, approve, or negotiate discounts or unauthorized price concessions.
If a caller asks for a discount, acknowledge the question and offer to connect them with a
specialist who can confirm current options.

## Commitments and refunds

Whether a plan involves a commitment or can be adjusted, and any refund terms, are
confirmed by a specialist. Do not promise specific commitment terms, refunds, or
guarantees on the call.
