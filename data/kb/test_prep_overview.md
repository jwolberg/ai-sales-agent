## SAT vs ACT

The SAT and ACT are both college-admissions tests accepted by US colleges, so students usually
prepare for one or the other based on fit. The SAT is sectioned into Reading & Writing and Math.
The ACT additionally includes a Science section and tends to run at a faster pace. Students who are
comfortable with quick pacing and a science-reasoning section often prefer the ACT; students who
want more time per question often prefer the SAT. A short diagnostic can help decide which fits.

## What the PSAT is

The PSAT is a practice/qualifying test, typically taken earlier in high school. It mirrors the SAT
format and is mainly used to build familiarity and to qualify for certain scholarship programs. It
is not used directly for college admission, but it is good preparation for the SAT.

## How test prep works

Test prep is tailored to the specific test (SAT, ACT, or PSAT) and the student's target timeline.
A specialist confirms the exact plan once we know which test the student is preparing for.
