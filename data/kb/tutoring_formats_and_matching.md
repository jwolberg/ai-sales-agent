## Formats

Tutoring is primarily delivered online in live, one-on-one sessions, so a student can
work with a great tutor regardless of location. Sessions are interactive and led by the
tutor in real time.

## How tutor matching works

We match a student to a tutor based on the subject or test, the student's goals and
level, scheduling needs, and the kind of support style that fits them best. The intent
is a tutor who fits both the academic need and how the student likes to learn.

## Changing tutors

If a match isn't the right fit, a student can be re-matched with a different tutor. If a
caller asks about a specific re-match or guarantee, confirm the current policy with a
specialist rather than promising specifics on the call.
