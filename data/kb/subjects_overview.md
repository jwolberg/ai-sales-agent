## Math tutoring

Math tutoring covers core high-school subjects, most commonly algebra and geometry. Sessions are
one-on-one and built around the student's current class, homework, and any specific topics they're
stuck on (for example, solving equations in algebra, or proofs and area/volume in geometry).

## Science tutoring

Science tutoring covers chemistry, biology, and physics. A tutor works through the student's
coursework and problem sets — for example, stoichiometry and bonding in chemistry, cells and
genetics in biology, or motion and forces in physics — and helps build problem-solving habits.

## What a tutoring session involves

Sessions are live and one-on-one, online. The tutor reviews where the student is, works through
current material and practice problems, and leaves the student with next steps. The specific plan
(subject, frequency, and tutor match) is confirmed with a specialist once we know the subject.
