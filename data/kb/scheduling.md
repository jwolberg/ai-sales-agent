## Flexibility

Sessions are scheduled around the student's availability — many families use weekday
evenings or weekends. Tell us what times tend to work and we factor that into the match.

## Frequency

How often a student meets with a tutor depends on their goals and timeline. A specialist
can help map out a cadence once we understand the situation.

## Rescheduling and cancellations

Specific rescheduling and cancellation rules are confirmed by a specialist. Do not quote
exact deadlines or fees on the call; offer to connect the caller with someone who can
confirm the current policy.
